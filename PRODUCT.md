# Draft Duck

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Newer and experienced fantasy basketball category-league managers preparing a build and consulting rankings during a draft.

## Product Purpose

Turn category priorities into a ranked player board. Help users find a build, configure their league, and understand why players fit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

CSV-backed season statistics, weighted category z-scores, named and custom builds, guided build discovery, optional draft-slot references, and player explanations. Rankings and displayed raw-stat modes are independent. Profiles stay on the device; no accounts, live league imports, pick tracking, or availability predictions. TanStack Start, React, Fastify, and the shared TypeScript core remain the stack.

## Brand Commitments

Draft Duck. Light but memorable duck personality through “Get your ducks in a row,” “Pick your punt,” and occasional quack copy. Use a text wordmark; do not generate logos. Statistical labels and errors stay literal. Punting is optional.

## Product Principles

- Guide first-time users; keep direct paths for experienced users.
- Explain category tradeoffs with actual ranking data.
- Reveal numerical controls when requested.
- Keep statistics directly accessible on every screen size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, labelled charts, and generous touch targets.
