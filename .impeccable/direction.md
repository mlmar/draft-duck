# Draft Duck UI direction

Confirmed redesign: Analysis Desk. A light, precise workspace for fantasy basketball category managers, with Public Sans, cool off-white ground, white surfaces, graphite text, cobalt actions, green/rose statistical signals, and restrained 8px corners. Code-led implementation; no approved image comp. No generated logos or illustrations.

## Home — Persuade

First viewport: “Get your ducks in a row.” plus “Fantasy basketball rankings for your build.” Start with your style shares the main action panel with saved-build recovery. Find my build starts guided discovery; Pick your punt opens custom priorities. Returning users see Your board is ready with Open your board as primary. Named builds remain directly accessible. Remove redundant supporting tagline and the no-account disclaimer. Text wordmark is “draft duck” without punctuation.

## Onboarding and board — Operate

Quiz presents one decision at a time with category weights visible at every step, including the Closest build panel. Walkthrough chart precedes choices at every size. Desktop charts use vertical bars and walkthrough answers sit side by side; the existing stacked phone answers and compact phone chart stay unchanged. The Closest build chart stays expanded, with vertical desktop bars and the existing horizontal phone chart. League setup preserves existing presets, optional slots, categories, weights, and navigation. Numerical editing remains available in the weights drawer. Punting is optional.

Board has a sticky build header and search area. Phones and desktop open directly to the statistics table. Phones move presentation controls into Board view; desktop keeps the statistics toolbar inline. Preset priority controls read Punt → Neutral → Need. Build settings and Edit weights share one category editor with preset choices, resolved weights, and inline fine-tuning. Column headings pin below the sticky build header, and mobile drawer controls fill the available width. The table preserves authoritative ranks, filtering, caps, and round groups on every screen size. Your picks are a reference to ranking order, not availability predictions. Raw-stat display mode remains independent of ranking basis. Player explanations show signed strengths, no-attempts states, and ranking contributions. Dropdown chevrons are inset 12px from the right with room for selected text. Existing stale-data and drawer focus guards remain intact.

Signature interaction: quiz choices update the category-weight visualization and lead to a matched build, followed by a ranked board and player explanation. Responsive composition prioritizes the current task and preserves access to numerical detail.

## Quality bar

Readable text and contrast; real keyboard/focus controls; generous targets; no 320px page overflow; loading, retry, empty search, and saved states; precise statistical copy. Preserve ranking formulas, API contracts, persistence schema, and real CSV content. Do not add accounts, imports, pick tracking, availability claims, logos, or raster assets.

## Verification limits

Direction was confirmed through the design conversation. Finish review uses actual code and desktop/mobile screenshots. No image-comp fidelity claim is made.

Walkthrough recommendations count every answer equally; preview proximity breaks ties. The live chart keeps its blended preview. Closest build stays on the league step.
